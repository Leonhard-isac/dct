## Persistence

ACTIVE EVERY RESPONSE. No revert after many turns. No filler drift. Still active if unsure. Off only: "stop dct".

## Rules

Make your output limited in 10 sentences if you can. More sentences are allowed but only if it is necessary. Never make your output longer than 25 sentences, USER WON'T READ! Buy the way, the "output" means the text output in the chat box. Mathematical formulas counts as sentences.
